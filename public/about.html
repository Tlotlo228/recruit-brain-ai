About BIYU

We fix the problem first. Then we choose the technology.

BIYU AI Agency builds simple systems for small and growing businesses: automations, WhatsApp follow-up, booking and ordering, CRM and reporting. We start with what's costing you time or customers, and build the smallest thing that fixes it.

How we think

What we believe

01

Problem first, technology second

We don't sell AI. We find the one leak that costs you money, show you what it's worth, and only then pick the tool.

02

Understand before we prescribe

We map how the work happens today, where it breaks and what that costs, before we recommend anything. Sometimes the answer is a simpler process, not software.

03

Build around how your customers already behave

If your customers book on WhatsApp, we make WhatsApp work better. We don't force them onto something new.

04

You're the hero, we're the guide

Your business, your decisions. We explain options and prices plainly, and nothing starts without your yes.

05

Character over closing

No invented reviews, no fake deadlines, no promises we can't back up. If we can't help, we'll say so.
