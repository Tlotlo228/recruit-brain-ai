Privacy

Who we are

Biyu Ai Agency Proprietary Limited, CIPA registration BW00009940055, Gaborone and Francistown, Botswana. Contact: info.biyu.ai@gmail.com. We are responsible for the details you share with us through this website.

What we collect

  • What you tell us on the booking form: your name, business name, WhatsApp number, email, type of business, country and town, how your customers reach you, the problem you want to fix, and whether you prefer a call or WhatsApp chat.
  • If you use the cost calculator, the numbers you entered, so your consultant can prepare.
  • Which advert or link brought you here (for example, campaign tags in the web address), so we know which adverts are worth paying for.
  • What you share with us during your consultation and any work that follows.

Why we use it

  • To arrange and deliver your consultation, confirm your payment and send you your summary or roadmap.
  • To reply to you and keep a record of what we agreed.
  • To send you practical emails about growing your business, only if you ticked the optional box. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which adverts bring in real enquiries.

Who can see it

The BIYU team and the consultant assigned to you. Your details are stored in the tools we use to run the business: our email (Google Gmail) and our workflow system (n8n, hosted on Hostinger servers). We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case we keep what we need for as long as we work together and as the law requires (for example, invoices). You can ask us to delete your details sooner at any time.

Cookies and tracking

We use one optional tool: the Meta Pixel, which tells us which of our Facebook and Instagram adverts lead to enquiries. It only runs if you press Accept on our cookie banner. If you press No thanks, nothing is tracked. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

If you accept and then book, we tell Meta that an enquiry happened (with your booking reference, not your name). The booking form keeps your answers only in your browser until you send them.

Other services this site uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this site, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Vercel hosts this website. n8n (on Hostinger servers) receives the booking form, and Gmail sends our emails.
  • Links to client websites open those sites in a new tab. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, delete them, or stop using them for marketing. Email info.biyu.ai@gmail.com and we'll respond. We follow Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information and Data Protection Commission of Botswana.

Terms

The consultation

  • The consultation costs P250 (Botswana Pula), paid before it takes place. We send payment details after you book. The amount and your payment reference are in that email.
  • You choose how it happens: a 30-minute call (phone or video) at a time you pick, or by WhatsApp chat in your own time.
  • You leave with a clear next step for your business.

Refund policy

If you finish the consultation without a clear next step for your business, tell us within 7 days at info.biyu.ai@gmail.com or on WhatsApp, and we refund the full P250. No forms, no arguments. Refunds go back the way you paid (Orange Money, Pay to Cell or bank transfer) within 7 working days of your request.

For roadmaps and builds, the refund and cancellation terms are written into the agreement you approve before any work starts.

Roadmaps and builds

  • The AI Roadmap costs P1,500 and is offered after a consultation when it's the right next step.
  • For anything we build, we agree the scope, price and timing with you in writing before work starts. Nothing starts without your yes.

What we can and can't promise

We give honest advice based on what you tell us and what we see. We don't promise specific revenue, savings or customer numbers, because results depend on your business, your market and how the work is carried out. Any estimates (including the cost calculator) are estimates, not guarantees.

Images, videos and the people in them

Screenshots on our Work page show websites and systems we built. We only name clients who have agreed to it. Mentee videos are shared with each mentee's consent. If you appear on this site and want something removed, email us and we will take it down.
