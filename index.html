Questions

Before you book

Why do you charge for the consultation?

The consultation takes real time to prepare and deliver. Charging a small fee means every consultant gives your business their full attention.

How long is it, and where does it happen?

30 minutes on a phone or video call, at a time you choose once payment is confirmed. Rather not talk? Choose WhatsApp chat on the form: you answer the same questions in your own time and get your summary in writing.

What if it doesn't help?

If you finish the consultation without a clear next step for your business, tell us within 7 days and we refund the full P250. No forms, no arguments.

We're not in Botswana. Can we still book?

Yes. Consultations happen by call or WhatsApp, so it works wherever you are. Outside Botswana we send you international payment options.

Do I have to buy anything afterwards?

No. If a written roadmap or a build would help, we explain the options and the price, and you decide. There's no pressure either way.

What if AI isn't the answer for my business?

Then we'll tell you. Sometimes the fix is a simpler process, a WhatsApp template or a change in who does what. We'd rather recommend the smallest thing that works than sell you technology you don't need.

Is this only for big companies?

No. We work with small and mid-sized businesses in Botswana and beyond, from salons and restaurants to consultancies and print shops.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to arrange and deliver your consultation. If you tick the optional box, we'll also send you occasional emails, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell or share your details. You can ask us to delete them at any time by emailing info.biyu.ai@gmail.com. This follows Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024. Full details: Privacy & Terms.
